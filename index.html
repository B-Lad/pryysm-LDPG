The Problem

Running a print farm with spreadsheets breaks at scale

Most additive manufacturing teams still juggle print requests in email, track filament stock in spreadsheets, and schedule jobs on whiteboards. When you scale past a handful of machines, the chaos compounds — missed deadlines, material stockouts, and zero visibility into OEE.

  • × Disconnected quality workflows

Pryysm MES unifies print request intake, fleet scheduling, material inventory, and quality control into a single platform. Built specifically for additive manufacturing — not adapted from generic MES software.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is an Additive Manufacturing Execution System (MES)? +

An Additive Manufacturing Execution System (MES) is specialized software that manages and optimizes 3D printing operations. It handles job scheduling, printer fleet monitoring, material inventory management, quality control workflows, and production traceability — specifically built for FDM, SLA, and SLS technologies rather than adapted from traditional manufacturing MES.

Which 3D printing technologies does Pryysm support? +

Pryysm supports FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling), SLA (Stereolithography), and SLS (Selective Laser Sintering). This covers the majority of industrial and professional 3D printers including models from Creality, Bambu Lab, Prusa, Formlabs, EOS, and others.

Can Pryysm handle multiple companies or departments? +

Yes. Pryysm is architected as a multi-tenant SaaS from the ground up. Each tenant operates in a fully isolated environment with PostgreSQL Row Level Security enforcing strict data boundaries. This makes it ideal for service bureaus, corporate R&D centers with multiple divisions, or educational institutions.

How does the AI Assistant work? +

The Pryysm AI Assistant analyzes your operational data to provide insights, anomaly detection, and recommendations. You can ask natural language questions like "What is my fleet OEE this week?" or "Which material will stock out first?" The AI can also suggest optimal job sequencing and flag potential quality risks before they occur.

Is my data secure? +

Security is core to Pryysm's architecture. We use JWT-based authentication with bcrypt password hashing, PostgreSQL Row Level Security for tenant isolation, and HTTPS encryption for all data in transit. Enterprise plans include SSO/SAML integration and dedicated infrastructure options.

Can I import data from my existing spreadsheets? +

Yes. Pryysm supports CSV import for materials, printers, user lists, and historical job data. Our onboarding team can also assist with migration from existing systems via our REST API. Most teams are fully operational within one business day.
